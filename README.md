# Information Security GRC Leadership Learning Path

This repository is a practical, scenario-led preparation programme for an enterprise Information Security Governance, Risk, Regulatory Compliance and Assurance (GRC) leadership role in a highly regulated BFSI environment.

The programme is based on the role brief provided with this project. It covers:

- Regulatory compliance and supervisory engagement
- Information security audits and assurance
- Closure and governance of audit and regulatory observations
- Enterprise security risk and control frameworks
- PCI-DSS and related security compliance requirements
- Policy governance, control assurance and regulatory readiness
- Executive and committee reporting
- Building and leading an Information Security GRC function

## How to use this repository

Work through the material in order. Each module has an outcome, preparation work, a field exercise, and evidence of completion.

1. Read [`docs/01-preparation.md`](docs/01-preparation.md) and complete the baseline assessment.
2. Follow [`docs/02-learning-path.md`](docs/02-learning-path.md) over six progressive stages.
3. Practise the cases in [`docs/03-scenarios.md`](docs/03-scenarios.md) without looking at the answer guide first.
4. Use [`docs/templates.md`](docs/templates.md) to produce executive-ready artefacts.
5. Review the role-readiness checklist in the learning path and retain your work as a portfolio.
6. Use [`docs/pci-dss-4.0.1/README.md`](docs/pci-dss-4.0.1/README.md) for the PCI DSS v4.0.1 knowledge base and banking assessment pack.
7. Complete [`docs/sox/README.md`](docs/sox/README.md) for SOX ITGC compliance before starting the [`docs/soc2-type-ii/README.md`](docs/soc2-type-ii/README.md) Type II assessment.

> **Important:** This is a learning aid, not legal or regulatory advice. Validate every regulatory interpretation against current RBI directions, applicable laws, card-brand rules, contractual obligations, and advice from the organisation's legal and compliance teams.

## Target learner

The content is designed for an experienced security, audit, risk, compliance, or technology leader preparing to operate at enterprise scale. It assumes familiarity with security fundamentals and focuses on judgement, governance, evidence, stakeholder management, and communication.

## Completion standard

You are ready to discuss the role when you can:

- Map obligations to accountable owners, controls, evidence, and testing frequency.
- Explain risk in business terms and make a defensible recommendation under uncertainty.
- Lead an examination or audit from request intake through evidence, issue management, and closure.
- Distinguish a temporary remediation, a compensating control, and sustainable root-cause treatment.
- Present a concise committee pack with decisions required, trends, ageing, and residual risk.
- Set up a GRC operating model with clear first-line, second-line, and third-line responsibilities.
