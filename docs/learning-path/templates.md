## Audit or regulatory observation

| Field | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Identifier and source | Which audit, examination, or review produced this observation? |
| Condition | What was observed, with factual evidence? |
| Criteria | Which requirement, policy, control objective, or commitment applies? |
| Cause | Why did the condition occur systemically? |
| Consequence | What could happen, and to which service, data, or obligation? |
| Severity and rationale | Why this rating, using the organisation's method? |
| Accountable executive | Who owns the risk and outcome? |
| Action plan | What sustainable change will address the cause? |
| Interim control | What reduces exposure until the permanent fix? |
| Target date and milestones | How will progress be tracked? |
| Closure evidence | What must be independently tested before closure? |
| Residual risk and acceptance | What remains, who accepts it, and until when? |

## 30-60-90 day leadership plan

| Period | Outcomes | Key actions | Dependencies | Measure of success |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Establish facts and trust | Listen, baseline obligations, open issues, critical services, and stakeholders | Access to records and leaders | Agreed baseline and governance cadence |
| Days 31–60 | Stabilise control and issue management | Set minimum standards, clarify ownership, triage risk, and improve evidence quality | Business and technology owners | Fewer unknown owners and defensible reporting |
| Days 61–90 | Institutionalise the operating model | Approve roadmap, assurance plan, metrics, team capability, and committee rhythm | Executive sponsorship and capacity | Decisions made from reliable risk information |
