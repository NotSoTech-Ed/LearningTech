## Scoring and review

This is a learning benchmark, not an ISACA passing-score calculator:

| Score | Study interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 80–90 | Strong readiness signal; validate weak domains and maintain timed practice. |
| 70–79 | Promising foundation; remediate errors before attempting another full mock. |
| 60–69 | Developing; revisit the knowledge base and complete applied cases. |
| Below 60 | Rebuild fundamentals by domain before using another timed mock. |

Calculate domain scores separately. A high total should not conceal a weak domain. For every incorrect or guessed answer, record the objective, error pattern, correct reasoning, and a reattempt date in [`evidence-register.md`](evidence-register.md).
