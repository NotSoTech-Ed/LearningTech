# SOX IT General Controls Compliance Pack

This pack provides a practical starting point for Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance over information technology general controls (ITGCs) supporting financial reporting. It is designed for a banking institution where financial applications, data, interfaces, infrastructure, cloud services, and outsourced providers may affect the completeness, accuracy, validity, or availability of financial reporting.

## Contents

1. [`knowledge-base.md`](knowledge-base.md) — SOX concepts, scoping, ITGC domains, control principles, and banking considerations.
2. [`assessment-method.md`](assessment-method.md) — risk assessment, walkthroughs, testing, deficiency evaluation, remediation, and management reporting.
3. [`assessment-workbook.md`](assessment-workbook.md) — control-family procedures, evidence requests, and management conclusion worksheet.
4. [`evidence-register.md`](evidence-register.md) — application inventory, control evidence, sampling, deficiencies, and remediation tracking.

## Important boundary

This is a control-readiness and internal assessment aid, not legal advice, an external auditor opinion, or a substitute for SEC/PCAOB requirements, applicable securities law, management's assessment, or the organisation's external auditor methodology. Confirm the reporting entity, fiscal year, material accounts, significant locations, service organisations, control framework, and auditor expectations before relying on a conclusion.

## Recommended sequence

1. Define the financial reporting risk universe and material systems.
2. Map significant accounts and disclosures to applications, interfaces, infrastructure, reports, data, and service providers.
3. Identify key ITGCs and application controls; document risk, control owner, frequency, evidence, and dependencies.
4. Walk through each control and test design before testing operating effectiveness.
5. Evaluate exceptions for severity, compensating controls, duration, aggregation, and impact on financial reporting.
6. Track remediation through sustainable operating evidence and independent retest.
