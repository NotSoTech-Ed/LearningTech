# SOC 2 Type II Assessment Workbook

Expand each row into the exact criteria and controls selected for the engagement. Retain evidence for the entire Type II period.

| Control family | Assessment procedure | Evidence request | Challenge prompts |
|---|---|---|---|
| System description and commitments | Reconcile description, architecture, contracts, customer commitments, and production inventory. | System description; architecture; service catalogue; contracts; change log; data flows. | Does the description include support, DR, cloud, vendors, and customer-impacting dependencies? |
| Governance and risk | Inspect policy approval, risk assessment, oversight, metrics, and issue escalation. | Policies; risk register; committee minutes; metrics; internal review. | Which risks exceed appetite and who accepted them? |
| Logical access | Sample provisioning, modification, termination, privileged access, and periodic reviews. | IAM/PAM reports; approvals; HR population; review sign-offs; termination samples. | Are customer and production access paths separated? Are break-glass events reviewed? |
| Physical access | Test facility authorisation, visitors, monitoring, environmental controls, and media handling. | Badge and visitor logs; facility reviews; CCTV; media records; supplier evidence. | Are cloud and colocation responsibilities clear? |
| Change management | Sample normal and emergency changes through approval, testing, segregation, and deployment. | Change tickets; code review; test results; deployment logs; emergency review. | Do changes to security, processing logic, and customer-facing APIs receive appropriate review? |
| Vulnerability and threat management | Inspect scans, penetration tests, remediation, threat intelligence, and exceptions. | Scan reports; penetration test; remediation tickets; exception register; alerts. | How are vulnerabilities prioritised and tracked to closure? |
| Incident response | Sample incidents and exercises; assess detection, escalation, investigation, recovery, and notification. | Incident tickets; playbooks; exercise results; post-incident reviews; notifications. | What triggers customer or regulator notification, and who decides? |
| Availability and resilience | Test monitoring, SLOs, capacity, backup, restore, DR, and outage communications. | Uptime/SLO reports; capacity; backup and restore; DR test; outage records. | Is evidence sufficient to prove commitments over the whole period? |
| Processing integrity | Trace transactions through validation, authorisation, processing, reconciliation, errors, and output. | Processing rules; interface logs; reconciliations; error queues; audit trails. | How are duplicate, missing, late, or unauthorised transactions detected? |
| Confidentiality | Test classification, encryption, access, retention, deletion, DLP, and disclosure controls. | Data inventory; encryption/key records; access reviews; deletion; DLP; contracts. | Where can confidential data leave the service boundary? |
| Privacy | Evaluate notice, rights, retention, disclosure, consent/lawful basis, and privacy incidents. | Privacy notice; processing register; request logs; retention; vendor clauses; incident records. | Are commitments consistent across regions and customer contracts? |
| Third-party and subservice management | Review due diligence, contracts, assurance reports, CUECs, carve-outs, and exceptions. | Vendor register; assessments; SOC reports; contracts; bridge letters; responsibility matrix. | Does each provider's evidence cover the service and period actually relied upon? |
