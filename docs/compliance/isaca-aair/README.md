# ISACA Advanced in AI Risk (AAIR) Learning and Exam Preparation

This track prepares experienced IT risk professionals to learn, practise, and assess the capabilities covered by ISACA's Advanced in AI Risk (AAIR) certification.

It follows the official AAIR structure of **90 questions across three domains**:

| Domain | Official weighting |
|---|---:|
| 1. AI Risk Governance and Framework Integration | 37% |
| 2. AI Life Cycle Risk Management | 21% |
| 3. AI Risk Program Management | 42% |

## Pack structure

1. [`knowledge-base.md`](knowledge-base.md) — AI risk concepts, governance, life cycle, controls, metrics, supply chain, and resilience.
2. [`learning-path.md`](learning-path.md) — a staged learning programme with practical deliverables.
3. [`assessment-method.md`](assessment-method.md) — how to test learning through case work and control assessment.
4. [`assessment-workbook.md`](assessment-workbook.md) — AI use-case, model, control, and risk-treatment assessment worksheets.
5. [`evidence-register.md`](evidence-register.md) — evidence, testing, findings, and action registers.
6. [`exam-preparation.md`](exam-preparation.md) — exam readiness, study cadence, original practice questions, and a mock-exam protocol.

## Official-source boundary

This is an original learning and assessment aid. It does not reproduce ISACA study materials, exam questions, answer explanations, or copyrighted content. Always confirm eligibility, exam policy, current content outline, registration, and official preparation resources at the [ISACA AAIR page](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aair) and [official exam content outline](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aair/aair-exam-content-outline).

The AAIR credential is intended for experienced IT risk professionals and requires an eligible professional designation. Confirm the current qualifying designations and other requirements directly with ISACA before scheduling.
