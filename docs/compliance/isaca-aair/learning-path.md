# AAIR Learning Path

## Stage 1 — Build the AI risk foundation

Learn the AI life cycle, risk vocabulary, use-case categories, data/model/system distinctions, and governance principles.

**Deliverable:** one-page glossary and a map of three AI use cases across ideation, validation, deployment, operation, and retirement.

## Stage 2 — Govern AI risk

Study accountability, risk appetite, policies, inventory, risk taxonomy, board reporting, independent challenge, and prohibited or restricted uses.

**Deliverable:** AI governance charter, RACI, risk-tiering method, and approval workflow.

## Stage 3 — Manage life-cycle risk

Trace data provenance, model validation, bias/fairness, explainability, privacy, security, robustness, drift, human oversight, and retirement.

**Deliverable:** a model/system record and life-cycle control matrix for one banking use case.

## Stage 4 — Run the risk programme

Practise scenario assessment, treatment selection, control validation, metrics, monitoring, supply chain, incident response, BIA, and DR.

**Deliverable:** AI risk register, treatment plan, KPI/KRI dashboard, and incident tabletop.

## Stage 5 — Apply judgement in cases

Complete the assessment workbook and explain decisions aloud. For each case, state impact, affected stakeholders, uncertainty, options, recommendation, owner, monitoring, and escalation.

**Deliverable:** five recorded briefings of five minutes each.

## Stage 6 — Consolidate for the exam

Review the official content outline by weighting: Domain 3 receives the largest study allocation, followed by Domain 1, then Domain 2. Use retrieval practice, timed questions, error logs, and mixed-domain revision.

**Deliverable:** completed exam-readiness dashboard with no recurring error category.

## Eight-week study plan

| Week | Focus | Practice |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation and terminology | Glossary retrieval and use-case mapping |
| 2 | Governance and framework integration | Charter, RACI, risk appetite, inventory |
| 3 | AI life cycle | Data/model/control walkthrough |
| 4 | Scenario identification and assessment | Threat and impact analysis |
| 5 | Treatment, controls, and metrics | Treatment options and dashboard |
| 6 | Supply chain, incidents, BIA/DR | Third-party review and tabletop |
| 7 | Mixed-domain cases | Timed original practice set and error log |
| 8 | Mock exam and final revision | Full timed simulation, remediation, rest |
