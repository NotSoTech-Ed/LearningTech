# AAIR Learning Assessment Method

## 1. Purpose

Test whether a learner can apply AI risk judgement in realistic governance and programme situations, not merely recall terminology. This is an original learning assessment and not an ISACA exam simulation or score predictor.

## 2. Assessment cycle

1. **Baseline:** complete the domain self-rating and explain one AI risk decision.
2. **Knowledge checks:** use short-answer retrieval after each learning stage.
3. **Applied assessment:** complete one banking AI use case through the full life cycle.
4. **Scenario test:** respond to cross-functional cases under time pressure.
5. **Mock exam:** use original questions with a timed protocol and error analysis.
6. **Remediation:** revisit weak concepts and repeat only missed objectives.
7. **Readiness review:** demonstrate consistent reasoning across all three domains.

## 3. Scoring rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Impact and stakeholders | Misses impact | Identifies a generic risk | Identifies affected parties and impact | Prioritises impact and explains trade-offs |
| Governance and accountability | No owner | Names an owner only | Defines decision rights and challenge | Aligns governance, appetite, and escalation |
| Life-cycle controls | Tool-only response | Partial control | Covers relevant life-cycle stages | Selects proportionate preventive/detective/corrective controls |
| Evidence and monitoring | No evidence | Activity claimed | Defines evidence and metrics | Defines thresholds, frequency, response, and assurance |
| Communication | Unclear | Technical description | Clear management response | Concise recommendation with uncertainty and decision required |

Target **12/15** per case and no score below 2 in impact, governance, or life-cycle controls.

## 4. Assessment workbook procedure

For the selected use case:

- Confirm purpose, users, decisions, affected people, and risk tier.
- Trace data, model, provider, technology, people, and process dependencies.
- Identify threats and failure modes.
- Select treatments and controls.
- Define validation, monitoring, incidents, BIA/DR, and retirement.
- Record evidence, residual risk, approval, and review triggers.

## 5. Integrity rules

Use original responses. Do not use recalled or leaked exam questions. Keep an error log that records the objective missed, why the answer was wrong, the correct reasoning pattern, and the date of successful reattempt.
